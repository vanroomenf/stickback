Community rules

Version of 28 October 2026

On Stickback you share an awkward moment and people reply with a sticker. We laugh with people, not at them. These rules apply to problems, replies, uploaded stickers, usernames and bios.

Who can use Stickback

  • You must be at least 16 years old.
  • One account per person. Don't pretend to be someone else.

What isn't allowed

  • Bullying and harassment: targeting, insulting, humiliating or threatening someone, even "as a joke".
  • Identifiable people: full names, photos, school, address or any detail that shows who you're talking about, unless that person agrees. Share your own awkward moments.
  • Hate and violence: content against people because of their origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or other characteristics; encouraging violence.
  • Explicit sexual content, and any sexual content involving minors.
  • Self-harm and suicide presented as something to copy or to mock.
  • Illegal activities, drugs, weapons, scams.
  • Spam: ads, suspicious links, repeated messages.
  • Other people's images you don't have the right to use, if the owner asks for them to be removed.

How we act

  • Every problem and reply has a ⋯ menu with Report and Block. Reports are anonymous.
  • Content reported by several people is hidden right away, until a moderator checks it.
  • New texts and images are checked by an automatic filter; the most offensive words are blocked.
  • Moderators review reports, usually within 24 hours, and can remove content or suspend accounts.
  • Anyone who breaks the rules repeatedly, or seriously, loses their account.

If someone is in danger right now, call 112 (911 in the US, 999 in the UK).
