We only collect the data Stickback needs to work. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

What data we use and why

DataWhy
Email and password (the password is only stored encrypted)Creating your account and logging you in.
Username, name and bioShowing your profile to other users.
Problems, replies, stars, uploaded and saved stickersRunning the app. Problems, replies and stickers used in replies are visible to other registered users.
Confirmation that you are at least 16 and accept the rulesEnforcing the minimum age. Your date of birth is checked on your phone and is not stored.

Legal basis: providing the service you asked for by creating an account and, for moderation, our legitimate interest in keeping the community safe.

Who processes data on our behalf

  • Supabase: database, accounts and image storage, on servers in Europe.
  • OpenAI: automatic moderation of texts and images.
  • GIPHY: searching and showing stickers and GIFs.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting the website and the web version of the app.

How long

We keep your data as long as you have an account. If you delete it, we erase your profile, problems, replies, stars, uploaded stickers, reports and blocks. They may remain briefly in backups, which are overwritten automatically.

Your rights

  • Delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  • Edit your name and bio from your profile, and delete your problems and replies from the ⋯ menu.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, its correction or restriction, by writing to [contact email].
  • Complain to a data protection authority: in Switzerland the FDPIC, in the EU the authority of your country.

Minors

Stickback is for people aged 16 and over. If we find out an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it.
